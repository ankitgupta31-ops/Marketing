Clear answers

Questions drivers
ask us first.

Can I apply for a new e-rickshaw loan?

Yes. Choose New e-rickshaw in the eligibility form and our team will explain the vehicle, down payment and repayment options available in your area.

Can I finance a cargo three-wheeler?

Yes. Cargo three-wheelers are included in our vehicle-loan focus. Your offer is based on the vehicle, documents, income profile and repayment capacity.

What documents should I keep ready?

Usually, applicants need identity and address proof, a bank statement or income details, and vehicle or dealer information. The exact list is shared during verification.
