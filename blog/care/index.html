Seven habits that extend your vehicle’s working life

Daily checks protect both your earnings and your passengers. Inspect tyres, brakes, lights, mirrors, and unusual sounds before starting your route. Keep the vehicle clean and do not ignore warning signs.

For electric vehicles, follow the battery maker’s charging guidance, keep connectors dry, and schedule servicing on time. Record repairs and routine checks so small issues are handled before they become expensive downtime.

As a practical rule, prevent small problems before they become large ones. If a light flickers, a sound changes, or service is due sooner than expected, treat it seriously. A consistent maintenance routine protects your daily income and keeps your vehicle on the road longer.

Reliable upkeep also reduces the stress of emergency repairs. When your vehicle stays healthy, you can keep your schedule stable, protect your reputation, and increase confidence in the business you run every day.
